Goal

Reflectile is a race to build beautiful islands of matching tiles. Bigger islands are good, but symmetric islands are worth much more.

Each player is assigned red or blue. Your score comes from islands in your color, but either player can place either color if their card allows it.

Watch both tile supplies. The game ends immediately when either color runs out, even if that happens on your opponent's turn.

Turn

On your turn, pick one card from your hand. You may play it on the board or discard it without placing a tile.

After the card is used, draw a replacement card and pass the turn. Discarding is always allowed, so you never have to make a move that helps your opponent.

UI

Click a card in your hand to choose it. Once a card is selected, click a legal position on the board to place the tile there.

If you do not want to play the selected card, click Discard. The last played or discarded card appears on the right side of the screen.

Hold and drag the board to move around the play area.

Reading A Card

Every card shows a tiny map. Dim tiles are the tiles that must already exist on the board. The full-color tile is the new tile you will add.

The color of the full-color tile tells you what the card places. A red card adds red, a blue card adds blue, and a wildcard lets you choose.

Red card adds red
Blue card adds blue
Wildcard chooses red or blue
Turn Or Flip

You do not have to match a card exactly as printed. You may rotate it or reflect it, as long as the dim tiles line up with matching tiles on the board.

These are the eight possible orientations of the same card. In each one, the dim tiles are already present and the full-color tile is the new placement.

Wildcards

Normal cards must place on empty spaces. Wildcards are special: they may replace an existing tile, then become the red or blue tile you chose.

Wildcards are the main way to repair a broken island. They can also disrupt an opponent's symmetry at the last second.

Scoring

At the end of the game, each color scores its islands. An island is a group of same-color tiles connected up, down, left, or right. Diagonals do not connect.

Islands with 1 or 2 tiles score 0. Islands with 3 or more tiles score points equal to the number of tiles in the island multiplied by the order of symmetry of the island, so a small perfect shape can beat a larger messy one.

The order of symmetry is the number of ways you can transform the island and get the same shape back. Check 4 rotations: 0, 90, 180, and 270 degrees.

Also check 4 reflections: vertical axis, horizontal axis, and the 2 diagonal axes. Symmetry can be x1, x2, x4, or x8.

x1
1 rotation + 0 reflections
Total score: 5 tiles * 1 = 5
x2
1 rotation + 1 reflection
Total score: 4 tiles * 2 = 8
x4
2 rotations + 2 reflections
Total score: 3 tiles * 4 = 12
x8
4 rotations + 4 reflections
Total score: 5 tiles * 8 = 40

Add together all scoring islands in your color. The player with the higher total score wins.
